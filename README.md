## What I'm building

I'm building [LipSyncTool](https://lipsynctool.com/), a browser-based
tool for creating AI lip sync and photo-based videos.

### Hotel Lobby AI

Create an AI rap duet from two portraits and style directions.
Turn yourself and a friend into the performers in a new musical video.

[Explore Hotel Lobby AI](https://lipsynctool.com/hotel-lobby-ai)

### Rumpelstiltskin AI

Personalize a fixed 6-second fairy-tale dance template with two portraits.
Choose a dancer and a spectator, then generate a video with
the template soundtrack included. Facial likeness and clothing
details may vary.

[Explore Rumpelstiltskin AI](https://lipsynctool.com/rumpelstiltskin-ai)
